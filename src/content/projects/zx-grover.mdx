## Overview

**ZX-Grover** applies ZX-calculus graph rewriting to Grover's search algorithm, reducing gate count and circuit depth without changing the algorithm's semantics or output distribution.

Presented at **AQIS 2023** (Asian Quantum Information Science Conference).
